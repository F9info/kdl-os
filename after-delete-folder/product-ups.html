Overview

Zero-transfer-time backup for what can't blink

A UPS is only as good as its sizing — too small and it can't hold the runtime you need, too small on battery bank and a five-minute grid dip becomes a hard shutdown. We size online double-conversion UPS to your actual critical load (server racks, POS systems, medical devices, essential lighting) and the runtime you actually need, not a generic VA figure.

Battery banks are sized and housed correctly for ventilation and life expectancy, and every installation is load-tested before handover.

  • Online double-conversion UPS from 1 KVA upward
  • Zero transfer time — critical loads never see a power blink
  • Battery bank sized to actual required runtime, not a generic figure
  • Correctly ventilated battery housing for rated battery life
  • Load-tested at commissioning, with AMC available
