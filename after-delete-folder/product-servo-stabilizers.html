Overview

Regulation tight enough to protect sensitive loads

CNC machines, medical imaging equipment and cold-storage compressors don't fail because of a power cut — they fail from the voltage swing right before or after one. Servo-controlled stabilizers correct voltage with roughly ±1% regulation accuracy, far tighter than a static or relay-type stabilizer, and we size them to the connected equipment's inrush current, not just its running load.

Every unit is installed with proper bypass arrangement so maintenance never means downtime, and we commission with a load test before handover.

  • Single-phase and three-phase servo-controlled units
  • ≈±1% output regulation accuracy — tighter than static/relay types
  • Sized to equipment inrush current, not just steady-state load
  • Bypass arrangement fitted so maintenance doesn't mean downtime
  • Load-tested at commissioning before handover

Why buy from us

Sized to inrush current, not just running load

A stabilizer sized only to running load trips or fails the moment your equipment starts up. We size on inrush current and fit a bypass, so servicing the stabilizer never means shutting down your equipment.
