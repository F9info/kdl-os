About Us

About Us

Your one-stop solution for building engineering products & services.

Subhadra Group was founded in the year 1996 with the aim to provide all building related engineering products & services under one roof for residential, commercial buildings and industries.

We provide complete solutions for HVAC, Electricals, ELV systems for residential, commercial and industrial applications. With 25+ years of technical expertise in these fields, our team strives to give perfect-engineered solutions for every application. Our team has experience of executing projects with leading MNCs and international groups with quality execution and on time delivery.

We deal with world-class brands that are pioneers in their respective fields to give the best solution to our clients. We have dedicated service managers for each field of services that we provide to give 24x7 support for all our installations.
