How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

Every hospital project is managed end-to-end by Subhadra Group's trained, in-house engineers — from load calculation and code compliance to installation, testing and handover. One team, one point of contact, fully accountable for the result.

Engineering Expertise

Every system is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard and to hospital safety codes.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site visit to final handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your hospital project — no subcontractors, no gaps in a critical environment.

We work with trusted brands such as Blue Star, APC, Fuji Electric and more. Our engineers are trained to follow the manufacturers' recommended installation standards, ensuring quality, reliability and long-term performance where downtime isn't an option.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so your systems are ready to perform from day one.
