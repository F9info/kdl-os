How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

A township runs on shared infrastructure — one gate, one perimeter, one power supply — so a gap in any single system affects every resident, not just one home. We design security, electrical, gate automation and clubhouse systems as one coordinated scope for the resident welfare association or builder, not as separate contracts per vendor.

Engineering Expertise

Every system is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site visit to final handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your township project — no subcontractors, no gaps.

The same approach carries to any gated community — CCTV and access control sized for every gate and block, electrical and DG backup for common areas, and one AMC covering the whole township. We work with trusted brands such as CP Plus, Schneider Electric, Cummins and Beninca.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so your systems are ready to perform from day one.
