How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, block after block.

Every builder project is managed end-to-end by Subhadra Group's trained, in-house engineers — from load assessment and product selection to installation, testing and handover on each block. One team, one point of contact, fully accountable for the entire programme.

Engineering Expertise

Every transformer, switchgear and DB is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard on every tower.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site visit to the final block's handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your project's MEP scope — no subcontractors, no gaps between blocks.

We work with trusted brands such as Schneider Electric, Megawin, RR Kabel, Polycab and more. Our engineers are trained to follow the manufacturers' recommended installation standards, ensuring the same quality and reliability on block one and block ten.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so snags and call-backs go to one number instead of getting lost between vendors.
