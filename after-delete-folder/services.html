Who's behind the work

30 years of building engineering, in Visakhapatnam

Subhadra Group was founded in 1996 to bring every building-related engineering service under one roof — for residential, commercial and industrial customers across Andhra Pradesh. These 15 services are designed, supplied, installed and maintained by that same team, not outsourced to sub-contractors.

With 25+ years of technical expertise across HVAC, electricals and ELV systems, we've executed projects for leading MNCs and international groups — Marriott, Novotel, NTPC, Vizag Steel and GITAM University among them — with a track record of quality execution and on-time delivery. Every discipline has its own dedicated service manager, so support doesn't end at handover.
