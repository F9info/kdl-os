Overview

Audio that's intelligible, zone by zone

A PA system that's just loud isn't the same as one that's intelligible — reverberant halls and long corridors both need speaker spacing and amplifier tap settings calculated for the space, not a single speaker type everywhere. We zone the system for independent announcements per area, add background music routing where needed, and integrate emergency voice-evacuation (EVAC) with the fire alarm panel where life-safety code requires it.

Every zone is speech-intelligibility checked at commissioning, and the EVAC integration is tested against the fire panel before handover.

  • Zone-wise announcement and background-music routing
  • Speaker spacing and amplifier taps calculated per space, not uniform
  • Emergency voice-evacuation (EVAC) integration with fire alarm panel
  • Speech-intelligibility checked per zone at commissioning
  • EVAC-to-fire-panel integration tested before handover
