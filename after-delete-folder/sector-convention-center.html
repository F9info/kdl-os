How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

Every convention center project is managed end-to-end by Subhadra Group's trained, in-house engineers — from load calculation and system selection to installation, testing and handover. One team, one point of contact, fully accountable for the result.

Engineering Expertise

Every system is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers for public-assembly occupancy, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site visit to final handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your venue project — no subcontractors, no gaps.

We work with trusted brands such as Blue Star, JBL, QSC, Bosch, Cummins and more. Our engineers are trained to follow the manufacturers' recommended installation standards, ensuring quality, reliability and long-term performance.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so your venue is ready to host from day one.
