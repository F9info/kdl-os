How we handle it

Compliant engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

Every government and public sector project is managed end-to-end by Subhadra Group's trained, in-house engineers — from specification and equipment selection to installation, testing, documentation and handover. One team, one point of contact, fully accountable for compliance and results.

Procurement-Ready Engineering

Every DB, panel, fire and security system is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, documented to government procurement and compliance standards.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site survey to final handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your public building project — no subcontractors, no gaps.

We work with trusted brands such as Blue Star, Schneider Electric, RR Kabel, Honeywell, Tyco, CP Plus, Matrix, Cummins and APC. Our engineers are trained to follow the manufacturers' recommended installation standards, ensuring quality, reliability and long-term performance.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so your facility is audit-ready and compliant from day one.
