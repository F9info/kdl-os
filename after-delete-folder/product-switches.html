Overview

A finish and rating for every room

Switches are the one electrical fitting a customer touches every single day, so we plan them at the same time as the interior — matte, glossy or metal finish, 6A general points and 16A/20A for AC and geysers, and combination plates that mix switches, sockets and USB charging in one frame.

Every plate is wired to a properly sized DB and circuit, not just clipped onto whatever wiring already exists — so nothing trips, nothing overheats, and everything is labelled for the next electrician who opens the box.

  • 6A, 16A & 20A modular ranges in matte, glossy and metal finishes
  • Child-safety shutters and anti-flicker LED indicators as standard
  • Combination plates with USB-A / USB-C fast-charge sockets
  • Point wiring and circuit sizing planned with your electrical layout
  • Genuine product with manufacturer warranty and original invoice
