How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

Every industrial project is managed end-to-end by Subhadra Group's trained, in-house engineers — from load study and equipment selection to installation, testing and handover. One team, one point of contact, fully accountable for the result.

Engineering Expertise

Every transformer, switchgear panel and stabilizer is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard.

One Point of Accountability

From the first load survey to final energisation, a single Subhadra Group team owns your plant project — no subcontractors, no gaps.

We work with trusted brands such as Kirloskar, Megawin, Transguard, Servomax, Fuji Electric, Cummins and Jakson. Our engineers are trained to follow the manufacturers' recommended installation standards, ensuring quality, reliability and long-term performance.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before energisation — so your plant is ready to run from day one.
