How we handle it

Expert engineering. One accountable team, start to finish.

A premium flat has less room for error than a standalone home — every conduit, point and unit has to be right the first time, inside a fixed floor plan and a builder's handover schedule. We plan air conditioning, electrical, automation, security and entertainment together, before the interior fit-out begins, so nothing is retrofitted or patched in later.

Engineering Expertise

Every system is sized and specified by our own qualified engineers, then installed to the manufacturer's exact standard.

One Point of Accountability

From the first site visit to final handover, a single Subhadra Group team owns your apartment fit-out — no subcontractors, no gaps.

The same approach carries to any premium flat — AC and wiring sized room by room, automation and security on one app, and a home theatre or multi-room audio setup that fits the space you actually have. We work with trusted brands such as Blue Star, Schneider Electric, Legrand and Sonos.

Every product is supplied with its original invoice and applicable manufacturer warranty, and every installation is thoroughly inspected and quality-checked by our team before handover — so your systems are ready to perform from day one.
